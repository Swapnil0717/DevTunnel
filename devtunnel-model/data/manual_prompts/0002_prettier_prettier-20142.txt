**Why?**

Follow up of https://github.com/prettier/prettier/issues/16710 / https://github.com/prettier/prettier/pull/16691

Prettier differs from CSS (and individual browsers) in how line breaks around CJ characters are recognized (whether they are equivalent to spaces or can be removed). Furthermore, processing methods are not standardized among browsers. Therefore, Prettier should keep line breaks around CJ characters as they are, even if proseWrap is set to something other than preserve.

The exception is line breaks between a CJ letter (excluding punctuation) and a Korean letter, where the behavior already matches that of CSS and individual browsers (equivalent to a space).

In https://github.com/prettier/prettier/issues/14936 / https://github.com/prettier/prettier/pull/16805, line breaks between CJK characters and non-CJK characters, as well as between CJK punctuation and Korean letters, will be allowed to be converted to spaces when proseWrap is set to always or never.

Related:

- dprint-plugin-markdown / deno fmt: https://github.com/dprint/dprint-plugin-markdown/issues/120#issuecomment-2426757408
